# Petridied

A game where when you die, you body becomes petrified.

Playing as a girl who can switch consiousness from one body to another, you have to reach the end of the 15 puzzles by burying yourself in a grave.

I really wish I had more time to work on this, as my college exams collides directly with the days of the jam.
As a result, there are some funny bugs and unstability and a huge lack of levels. You can see the controls in the game.
